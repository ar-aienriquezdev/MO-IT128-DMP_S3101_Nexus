NOTES / RATIONALE
================================================================================
[NOTE] Why redundancy trimming matters:
- Redundant features inflate correlated signals and can make distance-based clustering less stable
- Trimming improves interpretability and often yields cleaner cluster boundaries

[NOTE] Why PCA was applied before K-Means:
- PCA compresses correlated features into orthogonal components
- Helps K-Means perform more reliably (centroid-based distance clustering works better with reduced multicollinearity)

[NOTE] Why k selection is not “metrics-only”:
- In real customer segmentation, the best “math” k can produce segments that are too broad (k=2)
  or too granular (micro-segments), reducing business usability.
- We choose k that balances quality metrics with actionability and stakeholder interpretability.
